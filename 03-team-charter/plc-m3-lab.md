# Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster, Module 3 Lab

## Name the situation
- **Who they are (role, not name), what you have observed, and how long it has been happening.:** A team member is frequently behind deadlines, frequently absent, on medical leave, shows initiative when confronted, but slips back into the same patterns as time goes by.

## Make a diagnosis
- **Your diagnosis, plus one sentence on why. Is your frustration with their behavior, or with a decision you made?:** Hiring mismatch: growing evidence this person is wrong for the role, not a feedback or clarity issue. Feedback and Performance improvement plan was put in place, yet the behaviour has not changed.

## Write your opening line
- **One next action I will take in the next two weeks is:** Review the Formal Performance Improvement Plan to specifically identify the gaps and issues in their performance. Calling out absenteeism and delays on deliverables that have lead to escalations and material delays on projects.
- **The first sentence of the conversation I need to have is:** I’d like us to review the commitments we agreed on, the progress made, and the areas where expectations remain unmet. I also want to hear your perspective before we discuss next steps.

## AI role-play
- **After you step out: what did the role-play change about how you will open this conversation for real?:** I changed my opening line to include hearing from the employee in review. it puts them into an objective view of the situation and to be able to call out something that was missed.

## Refine and complete your charter
- **What We Own. What this team owns.:** End-to-end onboarding, retention, and AI-driven personalisation to improve post-acute engagement (120–180 days), customer well-being, and long-term retention.
- **What is out of scope.:** B2B expansion (Fable for Teams), social networking, clinical services, and new platforms or markets outside the approved strategy.
- **Cross-boundary decisions that need a joint call.:** Changes affecting shared customer journeys, AI capabilities, consent, or resources require alignment between relevant PMs, Growth, and technical leads.
- **How We Decide. Who decides feature and scope calls.:** Each PM owns decisions within their domain; the Product Leader makes final calls on strategic priorities, resource allocation, and competing OKRs.
- **How cross-team conflicts escalate.:** PMs attempt resolution within 1 working day; unresolved conflicts escalate to the Product Leader for a binding decision within 3 working days total.
- **Who resolves escalations from outside the team.:** The Product Leader is the single point of accountability, escalating material budget or strategic decisions to the executive sponsor and safety/compliance matters to authorised specialists.

## Show and swap your team charter
- **Where does the charter leave room for interpretation that could cause a conflict?:** The charter does not clearly define final decision rights when ownership overlaps, particularly between Onboarding, Retention, AI Personalisation, and Growth. Ambiguity around shared resources, approval authority, and escalation thresholds could create competing priorities, delayed decisions, and accountability gaps.
