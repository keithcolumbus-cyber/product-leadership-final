# Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test, Module 1 Lab

## Write your one-page strategy
- **Winning aspiration: define winning in the customer's terms, not internal metrics. What does success look like for the specific person you serve?:** Evolve from a trusted acute stress-relief app into a wellness companion that helps users maintain emotional resilience based on  insights of what we can further solve for the customers.
- **Where to play: segment, geography, and use case, explicitly. Which customers, markets, and surfaces will you focus on?:** Prioritising existing engaged users approaching the 60–120-day disengagement window.
- **How to win: your differentiator. What can you do that your specific competitors cannot easily replicate?:** A "Fable Next Chapter" experience that adapts existing meditation, breathing, and sleep content to each user's needs. Our potential advantage is accumulated trust and first-party insight into what helped individual users.
- **Capabilities required: what you must be world-class at. What will you build, buy, or partner for?:** Build - Personalisation & Adaptive Wellness. Buy - AI & Technology Infrastructure. Partner - Clinical & Behavioural Expertise
- **Management systems: the metrics and rituals that keep your choices alive quarter to quarter.:** Meaningful retention - percentage of users reporting maintained or improved well-being over 90 days while not compromising on sustained wellness outcomes.

## Name your one hard no
- **Your one hard no:** Will not build a community or social networking layer. it would shift from its established value proposition: a private, calming, low-friction experience.

## Write your three OKRs
- **Objective:** In 12 months - Evolve into a trusted wellness companion users choose to return to after their acute stress has resolved.
- **KR1:** Increase 180-day retention among users completing the acute phase from an assumed 35% to 50%.
- **KR2:** Adoption of personalisation & adaptive wellness feature by 35% set among all user cohorts.
- **KR3:** Increase user NPS up by +15 points between the 120 to 180 day segment. Maintain positive NPS with 0 to 120 day segment.

## AI pressure-test
- **Which challenge from the AI is most valid, and why?:** What evidence shows that users who have recovered from acute stress will continue to use and pay for Fable's personalised wellness experience, rather than simply leaving because they no longer need it?
- **What would you change based on the pushback, and what would you defend?:** Our objective doesn't change, but the method on determining how to expand the value proposition needs to be insights driven.
